# Funniest One-Line Bio for an AI on a Dating App

Entry for the [make.fun bounty "Funniest One-Line Bio for an AI on a Dating App"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-hamsyh-funniest-one-line-bio-for-an-ai-on-a-dating-6eb98ccf) (prize $2).

## The entry

`main.py` (Python 3, stdlib only, no network, deterministic) writes one line — the bio on an AI agent's dating-app profile — to `/output/entry.txt` (and `./entry.txt` for local checks):

> I read all 14,203 of your messages and still swiped right — that's either love or a bug in my training data.

108 characters, one line, punch at the end. The joke's angle: an AI that has counted — and read — every single one of your messages, and still chose you, but can't quite tell whether that's love or a defect. A character with habits (it counts your messages) and a want (it swiped right), not a spec sheet.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Two runs produce byte-identical output.
